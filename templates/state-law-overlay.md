# State Privacy Law Overlay (supplement to HIPAA)

HIPAA is federal floor. If you serve patients in these states, layer requirements below.
**Have counsel review** before relying on this checklist.

| State / law | Trigger | Key additions beyond HIPAA | Your status |
|-------------|---------|----------------------------|-------------|
| **California — CCPA/CPRA** | CA residents | Consumer rights (access/delete), sensitive PI categories, service-provider contracts | TBD |
| **Texas — HB 300** | TX residents | Training, ePHI access controls, breach notice to TX AG | TBD |
| **New York — SHIELD Act** | NY residents | Reasonable safeguards; breach notice to NY AG | TBD |
| **Washington — My Health My Data** | WA consumers | Consent for health data collection/share; geofencing limits | TBD |
| **Colorado — CPA** | CO residents | Privacy rights similar to CCPA for personal data | TBD |
| **Connecticut — CTDPA** | CT residents | Consumer privacy rights | TBD |
